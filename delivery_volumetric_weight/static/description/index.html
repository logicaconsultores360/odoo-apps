Delivery Volumetric Weight

Quote shipping on the weight your carrier actually bills

Odoo 19 · Community & Enterprise

The problem

Carriers do not charge for the weight of a parcel. They charge for the chargeable weight: whichever is greater between the real weight and the volumetric weight derived from the parcel's dimensions. A box of pillows weighs four kilos and occupies the space of forty — and the carrier invoices the space.

Odoo's delivery price rules only know about Weight, Volume, Price and Quantity. Bulky-but-light orders are therefore quoted far below what they will actually cost you, and the difference comes straight out of your margin, order after order.

The fix

This module adds a new variable to delivery price rules — Effective Weight — computed exactly the way carriers compute it:

Effective Weight = Σorder lines max( real weight , volumetric weight ) × quantity
Volumetric Weight = (Length × Width × Height) / Volumetric Divisor

Point your carrier's price rules at Effective Weight instead of Weight and the quote your customer sees matches the invoice your carrier sends.

Features

  • Product dimensions — Length, Width and Height in centimetres, on a dedicated Dimensions tab of the product form.
  • Per-product divisor — 5000 by default (road and express), 6000 for air freight, or whatever your contract says. Set it to 0 to exclude a product.
  • Stored, indexed field — the volumetric weight is recomputed automatically and can be filtered, grouped and reported on.
  • Mass update — both fields are available as optional columns in the product list view, so you can fill in a whole catalogue in minutes.
  • Any carrier — works with every delivery method based on price rules, including your own custom ones.
  • Zero regression — existing rules based on Weight, Volume, Price or Quantity keep behaving exactly as before.

How to use it

  1. Fill in the dimensions

    Open a product and go to the Dimensions tab. Enter length, width and height in centimetres. The volumetric weight is computed as you type.

  2. Check the divisor

    The default is 5000. Change it on the products shipped under a different agreement — for a whole catalogue, use the optional column in the product list view.

  3. Update your price rules

    Go to Inventory → Configuration → Delivery Methods, open a carrier, and in the Pricing tab set the rule variable and factor to Effective Weight.

  4. Quote as usual

    Add delivery to a sales order. The shipping cost is now computed on the chargeable weight, exactly like your carrier's own rate calculator.

Technical notes

The effective weight is computed in delivery.carrier._get_price_available() and injected through the context into _get_price_dict(), the hook Odoo explicitly documents for adding custom variables to delivery price rules. Nothing in the standard pricing flow is bypassed.

When a delivery price is computed outside a sales order — from a picking, for instance — effective_weight falls back to the plain order weight, so no rule can ever fail for a missing variable. Service and combo products are excluded from the calculation, matching the standard Odoo behaviour.

Requirements. Odoo 19 (Community or Enterprise) with the delivery module installed. No external Python library, no third-party dependency.
